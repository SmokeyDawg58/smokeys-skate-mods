# Credits and AI disclosure

## Smokey's Skate Mods

**Smokey** directed the project, chose and refined features, supplied branding and inputs, and tested the mod with friends and community contributors. Community contributors supplied patches, feedback, weather research, and source material. Daytryan's work informed the isolated lightning adaptation. Names are included where the source material and notices identify them; please open an issue if a credit needs correction.

**AI was involved throughout this project.** OpenAI ChatGPT/Codex assisted with research, implementation, debugging, tests, and documentation under Smokey's direction. AI assistance does not replace credit for the original research, upstream projects, or community testing, and a passing check does not establish full game parity.
