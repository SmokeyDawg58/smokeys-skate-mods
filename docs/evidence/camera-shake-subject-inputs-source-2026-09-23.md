## Speed operand graph

`82e48678` loads the **full** Rig subject-velocity vector and stores it to two stack slots. The vector passed to the length graph is separately built at `var_2B0`: `82e4868c..90` copies only subject velocity X to its X lane. Its Y was written `+0` at `82e4862c`; Z is copied at `82e48694..98` from `var_298`, which `82e48618` previously zeroed; W is zeroed at `82e4869c`. Hence the operand at `82e486a0` is `[mSubjectVelocity.x,+0,+0,+0]`. Raw `82e486a4 167ff9b5` is a self dot3 of that vector (IDA prints aliased `v95` for the actual v63 operand). The result is X² for ordinary finite inputs. `82e486a8..d8` issues one VMX reciprocal-sqrt seed and two fused Newton refinements, then multiplies the square by the refined reciprocal. `82e486b4/dc` selects exact +0 when the square equals zero, **after** issuing the estimate. `82e486e0` stores the length in stack `var_2C0`, read as `subjectSpeed` into FPR2 at `82e48744`. This is not full 3D speed or an X/Z horizontal speed; for ordinary finite inputs it is `abs(mSubjectVelocity.x)`. The existing `CameraRig::try_subject_speed` is signed along a heading and is not this graph. `reckoning_numeric::try_length` provides the same ordered dot3/estimate/two-refinement/zero-select host boundary when given the explicitly X-only vector.

## Wipeout, branch and distance

`82e486e8` calls Subject vtable `+0x17c` (`GetIsWipingOut`) and reads its referenced bool. If false, `82e48710` sets the shake activation flag to 1. If true, `82e486f8..82e4870c` tests `mLogicalCameraPositioner.mColliding`; activation is 1 when colliding, 0 otherwise. Thus `activate = !wiping_out || colliding`, with no other Subject reads. The flag is formed before `shotEditor` is tested at `82e48714`. `shotEditor=true` copies the staged new Rig basis and camera-position columns directly into `Frame.matrix` at `82e4871c..28` and skips both distance and ShakeEffect.

On `shotEditor=false`, `82e48758` loads `Rig.mAnchorMatrix` translation column `+0x30`; raw `82e48760 15aee077` subtracts the previously staged positioner camera position (`v124`), **anchor minus camera** in all four lanes. Raw `82e48764 158d69b5` self dot3s that difference; `82e48768..98` applies the same estimate/two-refinement/zero-select length graph. Its scalar is loaded as `subjectDistance` into FPR3 at `82e487a4`. W participates in the subtraction but not the dot3. `82e487a8` then calls `ShakeEffect::Apply(dT,subjectSpeed,subjectDistance,stagedRigMatrix,activate)`. The ShakeEffect owner reads parameters and loaded shake keyframes; it requires separate source/code review before a normal camera frame can be published.
