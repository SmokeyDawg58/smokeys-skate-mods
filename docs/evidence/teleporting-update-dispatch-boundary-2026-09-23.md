# Teleporting 702 first Update dispatch boundary

The mounted 300-frame raw bail replay enters Teleporting 702 at step 257, then fails in `Tick(Before(Reckoning(ForeignState)))`. This is a Rust dispatcher error: `physical_player_ground_pre_solve.rs` handled Air, WipeoutGround, GroundAnimation and OffBoard, then fell through to `try_update_pair_ground_reckoning` for every other state. Teleporting was therefore incorrectly sent through Ground.Update.

IDA `PhysicalPlayerHiLOD::Update3_State` `0x82dfe708` calls `mPhysicsState->Update` at `0x82dfe7d0`, after prediction, push-abort reset, board flag clear and grab sync. The Teleporting vtable's Update is `PhysState_Teleporting::Update` `0x82d86940..0x82d869b8`. It reads Processed `+0x8c4` bit `4` (`0x82d86944..50`). With that bit set, it copies all four columns of Processed external reset `+0x5c0` into the Teleporting reset transform, marks data acquired, copies external on-board and marks ready (`0x82d86954..b0`). With the bit clear, it calls `Skeleton::TeleportSomewhereAppropriate` at `0x82d869b8`. That wrapper delegates to the bound Actor query (`0x82b3b860..70`). `Actor::TeleportSomewhereAppropriate` `0x825f0e18..0x825f0f50` chooses AI respawn or a suggested breadcrumb and invokes `Actor::Teleport`.

The selected pre-solve now explicitly reports `TeleportingUpdatePending` immediately after the already mounted prediction and before Ground.Reckoning. It preserves the earlier per-frame writes and does not claim the native callback ran. The source-backed next producer for the observed automatic recovery is Actor respawn selection, followed by its external reset publication and a later Teleporting.Update.
