# Park editor and props across challenge types

Explicit PC mod policy requested by the owner, who also requested publication
after the change and short checks. This is not a retail parity claim.

Remove the editor refusal during active career challenges and online rounds,
and its forced close at an online round start. Retain ready-input, focus,
pause-menu and Options checks. No type or prop-category allowlist replaces
those restrictions. The shared catalog, model resolution, limits, collision,
grinds and physical board behavior remain unchanged.

Move dropped-prop recovery from the online-only DMO updater to the completed
shared gameplay tick, after career and online resets. Explicit recovery runs
only when native census spawning is disabled; an enabled census retains its
original distance and streaming rules. Placed props stay local. Remote prop
synchronization is not added.

Confidence: high for these explicit restrictions and common reset routing.
Signed validation distinguishes inspection and short actual EXE checks from
a full playthrough of every challenge. The approved Clean/Sketchy layout and
existing scoring/physics remain. Diagnostics use copied windowed saves,
inactive offscreen windows and audio dumps. Playing copies and volume remain.

Challenge trigger operations also clear, disable or save their area. Their
bulk DMO cull now skips editor-owned dynamic objects; their locator selection
and save lists exclude the reserved PCDR locator namespace. Thus Reset, Clear,
Save, restart and unwind cannot remove or move the editor placements. Authored
locators and challenge objects continue through their original operations.
