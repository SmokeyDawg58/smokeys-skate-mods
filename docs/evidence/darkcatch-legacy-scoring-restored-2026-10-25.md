# Owner-requested darkcatch scoring rollback

The owner explicitly requested the original mod behavior after repeated online
false cancellations: a held darkcatch landing must keep its points. Remove the
PC touchdown Guard, its Runtime cancellation, descriptor/flag suppression and
its additional host ScoreModule reset. Ordinary teleport and wipeout resets
remain. Darkcatch recognition, physical controls, and audio are unchanged.
This intentionally supersedes the earlier online rule and its landing guard
tests; physical support checks are no longer needed by this removed policy.
