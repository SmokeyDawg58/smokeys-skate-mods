# Contributing

Thanks for helping. For a bug or proposed feature, open an issue first with a clear reproduction or a short description of the change. Small, focused pull requests are easier to review.

- Preserve existing upstream and third-party attribution and license notices.
- Keep game assets, captured packs, saves, private logs, credentials, and machine-specific paths out of Git.
- Keep upstream behavior authoritative, and do not silently discard existing Skate 3 features to make a merge succeed.
- Explain the intended behavior, what was actually tested, and any known limitations. Avoid claiming exact parity without evidence.
- Follow the audio and scratch-profile guidance in [Building](docs/BUILDING-SMOKEY.md) and the upstream [contributor guide](AGENTS.md).
- Tell reviewers when AI assisted a contribution; maintainers remain responsible for understanding and testing submitted work.

For user-facing problems and privacy guidance, see [Support](docs/SUPPORT-SMOKEY.md).
